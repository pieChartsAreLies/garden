Why you feel so stuck in life. - Mark Manson ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=411yMnGP5Og)) - This is NOT my idea... it's not really resonated with me... will need to think of how I want to define life-stages. Couch it in career growth and progression. 
* Stage One: Copycat - Lots of people never leave it. We copy others, and at the end, we realize that we want to be our own person. Goal is to teach ourselves how to function in society. Adults help us to make decisions and take action for ourselves. Sometime we are punished for decisions. We get stuck in stage one mimicking others and trying to please everyone so we're not judged. Ideally, this ends at early adulthood. 
* Stage Two: The Explorer - Learning what makes us different than the people around us. Understand where our limits. Classic young person. Full of cringe and questionable life choices. Goal is a process of self-discover. We try things. Some go well. Som of them don't. The goal is to stick with what goes well and drop the ones we don't care about. We're trying to find out values. Living in alignment of values brings good stuff. ([Deep-Dive into Values](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvXdMPNhp9M&pp=0gcJCR0AztywvtLA)) It's okay to be bad at things. Learning what you aren't good at is part of this stage of our journey. Anything that's driven by pleasure and novelty, the less valuable it becomes. Time on this planet is brief. Spend it on things that matter to you...your values. If you get stuck here, oyu're like peter pan... life is short, not all dreams will come true. Recognizing that you have a limited amount of time... 
* Stage Three: Martyr - What is worth dying for? Double down on what you are best at. Double down on important relationships... then your life mission. Stage these is where you do these things. It ends when you are old and tired or you feel like there's nothing left to do. 30 to retirement. People get stuck in ambition, power and influence. 
* Stage Four: Death - Immortality projects? It's about letting go. A family, career, movement, success, failure,...."Did any of this actually matter?". Fear of death isn't about dying...but about being irrelevant. Those that get it right, they turn to passing things on, supporting, mentoring, etc. Influence in that way can be permenant because you can give away everything that you've built.
